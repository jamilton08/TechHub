Build the Final Boss

Someone broke into the Arcade's game engine. Make a character, walk the campus, and build the one thing that can stop him: a boss, one part at a time, out of everything you have learned.

A message is on every screen in Room 333:

> SNIPERSPAWN HAS CONTROL OF HSCT PROGRAMMING.
> NEW RULE: 50 ASSIGNMENTS PER DAY. ALL ASSIGNMENTS AUTO-FAIL.
> THE ARCADE IS MINE. GOOD LUCK.

The Arcade engine only listens to one thing: a boss. Build one that is complete — stats, moves, a brain, a save system, smooth movement — load it into the Arcade, and let it face him.

Make your character

  • 100 XP. Six parts of the boss (the gate, stats, moves, brain, save, motion) are 90 XP. Beating SniperSpawn in the Arcade is the last 10.
  • Every part is a programming puzzle at a desk. Your code runs for real, and the checks tell you exactly what is wrong.
  • Tech coins hide in tall grass and around the campus. Spend them at a hint kiosk (1, 2 or 3 coins for a bigger hint). Mentors in every zone explain for free. Every hint you buy is written in your result.
  • Walk with the arrow keys or WASD, or click where you want to go. Space, Enter or the A button talks, reads and sits down.
  • About 90 minutes. The clock starts at Start and stops when you finish. Need two classes? Save and continue later is under the map: your character, your coins, your code and your spot on the map come back.
  • At the end you download a result file and submit it in Google Classroom.
