Ten puzzles from Stat Check (every game is a database) and Smart Moves (build a game AI). Each puzzle walks you through it in three steps, so even if one step trips you up, you still see the logic and keep going.

    Rules

    • You have 25 minutes. The clock starts when you press Start, and the quiz submits itself at 0:00 with whatever you've done.
    • Steps in a puzzle unlock in order. You can skip to any puzzle with the numbers at the top and come back.
    • There's no running code: work it out in your head or on scrap paper, the way the computer would, one line at a time.
    • Leaving this tab and pasting into answers are both recorded in your result file. Refreshing the page erases everything.
    • At the end you download a result file and submit it in Google Classroom.
    Enter your name first. It goes on your result file.
