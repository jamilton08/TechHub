HSCT Spaceverse: distract the Space Ogre

Your team planned a game. Now you build it — in space, one station at a time, while a very bored ogre takes the place apart. Make a character, join your team, and fly.

Mission control, HSCT Spaceverse, 08:00:

> SPACE OGRE DETECTED ON THE DOCKING RING.
> HE IS NOT HOSTILE. HE IS BORED. THAT IS WORSE.
> HE HAS EATEN THE CAFETERIA, TWO AIRLOCKS AND THE WI-FI.

Only one thing holds a Space Ogre's attention: a game he hasn't played. Every team builds its Black Box game across the seven stations of the Spaceverse. Each finished part distracts him a little more. Launch the whole thing in the Arcade Dock and he sits down, plays, and leaves the Spaceverse alone.

Make your character

  • 100 XP, one result per team — the same seven parts and points as your Black Box plan: objects & state (15), win and lose (15), the black box (25), saves (15), three questions from the log (15), the enemy brain (10), the design doc and demo (5).
  • Every part is a console at a station. Your code runs for real, and the checks tell you exactly what is wrong. The Mission computer in mission control loads your black-box-plan.json so your objects, state keys and win/lose rules are already filled in.
  • Your team has four ships on the Docking Ring pad. Walk up to a free one and press E to fly it; any transporter by a hatch beams you to any ship. Space is for ships; stations are for feet.
  • Star credits hide in nebula dust — fly through it. Spend them at a hint console (1, 2 or 3 credits for a bigger hint). Mr. Cruz and Mr. O explain for free at every station. Every hint you buy is written in your result.
  • Walk with the arrow keys or WASD, or click where you want to go. Space, Enter or the A button talks, reads and sits down. F puts the Spaceverse on the whole screen.
  • Two classes. Your team's progress is kept on the server: close the tab, come back, join with the same code and name, and you are where the team is. One person per console at a time — if two of you type in the same console, the last one wins.
