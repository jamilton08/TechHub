About me: your GitHub profile

Everyone who looks you up online is asking the same question: who is this person and what can they do? Today you answer it on purpose — and then publish the answer on your own GitHub profile.

  • Four short readings, each with three questions. 5 points a question — 60 total.
  • One sorting round on what belongs on a public page. 16 points.
  • One draft. You fill in the About Me builder and it writes your README.md for you. 24 points.
  • Then the steps to hook it up to your GitHub account — including making one, if you don’t have one yet. 100 points in all.
  • The clock starts when you click Start. At the end you download a result file and submit it in Google Classroom.

The readings open in a new tab. If your Chromebook blocks one, open the grey box under it — the parts you need are quoted there.

Enter your name first — it goes on your result file.
