Team Up: the campus trail

Next project is a team game: four people, one jumping game, one grade. Before anyone writes code, you walk the campus trail. At every fork there is a situation a real team hits, and the path you take is your answer. Take the wrong path and you're sent back to the start of that fork. At the end of the trail is the Team Hall, where you put your team on the roster and give everyone a role.

Make your character

  • 100 XP. 15 at the Trailhead bulletin board, 60 on the trail (10 per fork: 10 for the right path first time, 5 on the second try, 2 after that), 25 in the Team Hall.
  • Walk with the arrow keys or WASD, or click where you want to go. Space, Enter or the A button talks to people, reads signs and uses desks. Read the sign at each fork before you pick a path.
  • Three roles on every team: Programmer, Presenter, Project Manager. You'll choose who does what in the Team Hall, and say why.
  • The clock starts when you click Start. Save and continue later is under the map; your character comes back exactly where you left them.
  • At the end you download a result file and submit it in Google Classroom.
