Job Shadow: a day at Bronx Byte Works

Bronx Byte Works is a small IT and software company on Fordham Road. Today you shadow its IT manager, Ms. Okafor, from sign-in to clock-out: a help desk ticket, a bug, an outage during a client call, lunch, a hard conversation with a customer, a team standup, and a debrief. After each stop you write one section of the HSCT Work-Based Learning Reflection. At the end you download it, filled in.

Make your character

  • 100 XP. Eight stops in the order of a work day. At each one: a scene with choices (2 XP per question on the first try, 1 on the second), then a section of the reflection worksheet (XP when it's long enough to say something).
  • Walk with the arrow keys or WASD, or click where you want to go. Space, Enter or the A button talks to people and sits down at desks. Ms. Okafor's plan decides which door opens next.
  • About 60–80 minutes. The clock starts at Start and stops when you clock out. Need two classes? Save and continue later is under the map: your character, your spot, and every word you wrote come back.
  • At the end you download two files: your result file for Google Classroom, and your reflection as a Word document (.docx), the school's worksheet filled in with what you wrote and a log of everything you did.
