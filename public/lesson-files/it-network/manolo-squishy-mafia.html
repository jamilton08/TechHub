OG Manolo's house: the Squishy Mafia

Manolo called. He doesn't call. "Mijo. Something is off." The house you wired in Part 3 works perfectly — and that is exactly the problem: it works for anyone who finds a way in.

What Manolo knows: a gray van with an antenna on the roof has been parked across the street for three days. Nena's work laptop got an email "from the bank." Junior "opened the cameras so he can watch them from anywhere." Last night something tried the camera recorder's login 400 times. And the tablet keeps joining a Wi-Fi called Casa Manolo that is not theirs.

The Squishy Mafia — three guys and a van — steal family photos and tax documents off home networks and sell them back. They case a house for a week first. They are on day three.

What has to stay private: the NAS (every photo since 1994 and Nena's tax and ID scans), the cameras, the Wi-Fi. Nena's budget for hardware: $350. Her car keys are on the hook.
