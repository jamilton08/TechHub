How the internet works.

You tap a link and a page appears from a computer on the other side of the world. Nothing about that is magic. In six short stops you'll see the whole trip: why every device has an address, why your data gets chopped into packets, how those packets find their way, how names become numbers, and how it all comes back together on your screen.

How this works

  • Six stops. Each one has an explanation, a diagram you can click and run, and one check question.
  • Checks are worth 15 points each; the last one, where you put the whole journey in order, is worth 25. That's 100.
  • Miss a question and you get one more try for half the points. Read the explanation and the diagram before you answer.
  • At the end you download a result file and submit it in Google Classroom.
Enter your name first — it goes on your result file.

That's the whole trip.
