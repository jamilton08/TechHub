Pop quiz: addresses, packets, and who’s listening.

Everything from the last three lessons: How the internet works, Set up the room and Packet Run. Quick checks, four packets written by hand, and two broken networks where you get the details and work out what’s really wrong.

Three parts, 100 points

    How this works

    • You have 15 minutes. The clock starts when you press Start, and the quiz submits itself at 0:00 with whatever you have.
    • You can change any answer until you submit. Everything you need for Parts B and C is on the page: read the ipconfig output, the config pages and the routing tables carefully.
    • Each problem in Part C ends with a short written answer. The computer doesn't grade it, but it's required and Mr. Cruz reads it.
    • Leaving this tab and pasting into your answers are both recorded in your result file. Refreshing the page erases your answers.
    • At the end you download a result file and submit it in Google Classroom.
    Enter your name first. It goes on your result file.
