Campus day one

It's your first day on the Room 333 campus. Walk around, talk to people, and get three things done: the whiteboard in the Team Hall, a note in the Workshop, then the Arcade when the other two are checked off.

Make your character

  • 50 XP: 30 at the whiteboard, 20 at the Workshop desk. The Arcade opens when both are done.
  • Walk with the arrow keys or WASD, or click where you want to go. Press Space, Enter or the A button to talk, read and sit down.
  • The clock starts when you click Start and stops when you finish.
  • Need more than one class? Save and continue later is under the map. Your character, your spot on the map and your work come back.
  • At the end you download a result file and submit it in Google Classroom.
Enter your name first. It goes on your result file.
